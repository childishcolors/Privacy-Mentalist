# Privacy Policy for Mentalist: Read Body Language

**Effective Date: May 20, 2026**

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how **Mentalist: Read Body Language** ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

The App does not use advertising networks or third-party analytics services. The App uses **RevenueCat** to manage and process in-app subscriptions.

### 1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or precise location.

The App does not require you to create an account, register, or log in.

However, when you use the App's subscription features, certain information related to your purchases and subscription status is processed by **RevenueCat**, our third-party subscription management service.

RevenueCat may process information such as:

* An anonymous App User ID generated by RevenueCat.
* Purchase and subscription information.
* Subscription status and entitlement information.
* App and device-related information necessary to provide its subscription services.
* App version and purchase-related dates.

We do not use RevenueCat to collect advertising identifiers for advertising purposes, and the App does not display advertisements.

### 2. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple's **App Store**.

Apple processes payment information associated with your purchase. We do not receive or store your credit card, debit card, or other payment information.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status, verify purchases, and provide access to subscription features. RevenueCat processes this information on our behalf as a service provider/data processor.

You can learn more about how RevenueCat handles data in its Privacy Policy:

[RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 3. Gameplay Data and Local Storage

Gameplay information such as your:

* Streak count
* Average score
* Games played
* Selected language
* Sound settings

may be stored locally on your device.

This gameplay data is not transmitted to our servers for analytics or advertising purposes.

You can erase locally stored gameplay data by using the **"Reset All Data"** option in the App's Settings menu or by deleting the App from your device.

### 4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service:

**RevenueCat** — used to manage and process in-app subscriptions and purchase information.

The App does **not** use:

* Advertising networks
* Third-party advertising SDKs
* Analytics services
* Tracking services
* Social media tracking SDKs

RevenueCat's processing of information is subject to its own privacy and security practices. RevenueCat states that it acts as a data processor when processing end-user information on behalf of its customers.

### 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Because the App does not require an account and does not use advertising or analytics services, we do not intentionally collect personal information for advertising or behavioral tracking purposes.

### 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed in connection with the App.

Subscription-related information is processed through RevenueCat and Apple's App Store infrastructure. RevenueCat states that application data is secured in transit using TLS and that customer data is stored using Amazon Web Services (AWS).

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights regarding personal information processed on your behalf, including rights to access, correct, or request deletion of certain information.

If you have questions about information processed through RevenueCat in connection with your use of the App, you may contact us using the information below. We may need to work with RevenueCat to fulfill applicable requests.
